Need urgent help?

KindPath is not an emergency service and cannot assess medical symptoms. If someone may be in immediate danger, has chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke, or another life-threatening problem, contact your local emergency service now. For urgent health concerns that are not immediately life-threatening, contact a doctor or local medical advice service.
